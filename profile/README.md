# ReeFrame.ai

**Open-source framework · In development**

### Your knowledge. Your tools. Your AI. Working for you.

**An open framework for building digital systems that learn, connect, and evolve without surrendering control.**

---

## Your tools are getting smarter. Your system isn't.

You have more computing power, more connected services and more AI at your disposal than ever before.

Yet every new tool comes with its own memory, its own workflows and its own model of your world. Your email knows your conversations. Your documents hold what you've learned. Your AI assistant can reason across extraordinary amounts of information.

But who connects it all? Who remembers why a decision was made, what happened afterward, and what should change next time?

**We have built increasingly intelligent tools without building equally intelligent environments for them to work in.**

## Not a harness. Not an assistant. The ground they stand on.

Harnesses and personal assistants connect an AI to your data. That is useful, but the agent sits at the center. Your context lives in its memory, under its provider's terms. When you switch, you start over.

ReeFrame inverts that.

| | Harness or assistant | ReeFrame |
|---|---|---|
| **At the center** | The agent | Your environment |
| **Memory lives** | In the tool or the provider | In substrate you own, portable by design |
| **Authority** | Whatever the agent is permitted | Governed: explicit boundaries, checkpoints, human decisions |
| **Change the model** | Start over | Plug in the next one, keep everything |
| **Learning** | Within a session | Accumulated from outcomes, over years |

**Harnesses and assistants are welcome here, as tenants, not landlords.**

## Four principles

**Connect what knows.** Bring applications, documents, services and people into useful relationships, without forcing everything into one app.

**Preserve what matters.** Context, decisions, provenance and lessons learned survive every change of tool, provider or model.

**Control what acts.** AI can observe, analyze, propose and execute within defined authority. Capability is not permission.

**Learn from what happens.** Connect actions to outcomes and improve deliberately. That means evolution informed by evidence, not uncontrolled self-modification.

---

## Built from practice. Designed for others.

ReeFrame grows out of a privately operated cognitive system that connects real applications, maintains persistent knowledge, governs AI-assisted work, and monitors the infrastructure beneath it.

The ambition is to turn that experience into an open, inspectable foundation. It will not prescribe one ideal stack or create another dependency. It will make it easier for people and organizations to assemble systems of their own and stay independent of the technologies they choose.

## An ecosystem should outlive its components.

A reef does not depend on one organism. Its strength comes from relationships, diversity and structure.

Applications will change. Models will improve. Providers will come and go. **Continuity should belong to the system's owner, not to its vendors.**

## Open foundations. Independent futures.

ReeFrame is being prepared as an open-source project, with reusable architecture, interfaces, operational patterns and reference implementations. The first public release will follow validation of its reference system.

Until then, this is an invitation to follow an idea taking shape:

**What if our digital environments became more valuable with every year we used them, and remained ours to evolve?**
